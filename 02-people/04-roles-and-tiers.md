# Roles and Tiers

ARGC has three roles: Member, Node Leader, and Super Peer. Each represents a different scope of accountability — from contributing within a node, to owning node operations, to carrying responsibility for the club as a whole. Advancement between tiers is based on XP and contribution activity, not seniority or time served.

## The Three Roles

### Member

The base role. A Member is an active contributor within a single node.

Responsibilities:
- Complete peer evaluations on schedule
- Participate in events
- Accumulate XP through verified contribution activity
- Communicate issues to their Node Leader

A Member's accountability is to their node. Their output feeds the node's record.

### Node Leader

A Node Leader manages the internal operations of one node and is accountable for its output.

Responsibilities:
- Own the node's evaluation schedule
- Track member contribution and flag issues early
- Escalate unresolved conflicts to Super Peers
- Report node status to the club level

Node Leaders are selected based on demonstrated performance as Members and the capacity to hold others to the same standard.

### Super Peer

The highest role in ARGC. A Super Peer operates at the club level — not within a single node.

Responsibilities:
- Oversee XP data integrity and club-wide metrics
- Manage voting processes for structural decisions
- Handle cross-node conflicts that escalate beyond Node Leaders
- Maintain the standards documented in this handbook

Super Peers are not managers. They are the people the system leans on when the normal channels are not enough.

## Tiers

Within each role, there are tiers based on cumulative XP and contribution consistency. Tiers determine:
- Visibility on the member platform
- Weighting in club voting
- Eligibility for leadership responsibilities

Tiers are reviewed on a fixed cycle. Advancement is automatic when thresholds are met. Regression is also automatic when output drops below threshold — a tier reflects current contribution, not past achievement.

## What You Cannot Buy

You cannot negotiate a tier. You cannot inherit a role. Seniority does not carry weight on its own.

If you were a Super Peer last cycle and did not produce this cycle, you are not a Super Peer this cycle. The system is designed to prevent prestige from outlasting the performance that earned it.
