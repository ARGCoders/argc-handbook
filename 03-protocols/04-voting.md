# Voting

Voting in ARGC governs decisions that affect the club's direction, membership status, and structural changes. It is not a consensus mechanism — it is a structured process with defined eligibility, quorum requirements, and documented outcomes. Verbal agreement is not a vote. Decisions that are not recorded did not happen.

## What Gets Voted On

Not every decision requires a vote. Votes are reserved for decisions with lasting consequences:

- Admitting a new member
- Removing a member or changing a member's tier outside the normal advancement cycle
- Amending this handbook or any core ARGC document
- Structural changes to how nodes are organized
- Club-level positions and their holders
- Any expenditure or commitment that binds the club externally

Day-to-day operational decisions do not require a vote. If a Node Leader can make a decision within their node's scope, they make it.

## Who Votes

Voting eligibility is tier-based:
- **Super Peers** — full vote on all matters
- **Node Leaders** — full vote on membership and structural matters; advisory on club direction
- **Members** — advisory input only, unless explicitly granted voting rights on a specific matter by Super Peers

Higher tiers carry more weight not because they outrank others, but because their XP record represents sustained investment in the club's outcome.

## How a Vote Works

1. **Proposal** — submitted in writing to Super Peers, with a clear description of what is being decided and why
2. **Review period** — minimum 48 hours for eligible voters to read and consider
3. **Vote** — cast on the platform, not verbally, not in a group chat
4. **Quorum** — a vote is valid only if the required percentage of eligible voters participate (threshold set by Super Peers)
5. **Outcome** — recorded on the platform with the vote count and the decision

## What "Recorded" Means

The record includes: what was voted on, who was eligible, what the outcome was. Individual vote choices are private. The outcome is not.

A decision that was made "in a meeting" without a platform record is not a club decision. It is an informal agreement between individuals.
