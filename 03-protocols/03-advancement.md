# Advancement

Members advance through ARGC's tiers by accumulating XP and meeting contribution thresholds, not by waiting. Advancement is reviewed on a fixed cycle — it is not requested, it is triggered by the data. Regression is built into the system: a tier reflects current contribution, and a sustained drop in output will lower it.

## The Review Cycle

Advancement is reviewed at the end of each contribution cycle. Cycle length is set by Super Peers and communicated to the club in advance — typically monthly or bi-monthly depending on club activity level.

At the end of a cycle:
1. XP totals are locked
2. Contribution records are verified against platform data
3. Members above threshold are advanced
4. Members below threshold are flagged for review
5. Members in sustained decline are regressed

The cycle is not negotiable. There are no extensions.

## Advancement Thresholds

Thresholds are set per tier and reviewed each cycle by Super Peers. They are not published in this document because they are subject to change — the current thresholds are always visible on the member platform.

What is fixed: the criteria categories (XP, evaluation completion rate, event participation). What varies: the exact numbers, adjusted to reflect what the club is producing in that period.

## How Advancement Happens

Advancement is automatic. If your data meets the threshold, you advance. You do not submit a request, make a case, or ask permission.

If you believe your data is inaccurate — an evaluation that was not logged, an event that was not credited — you raise it with your Node Leader before the cycle closes. Not after.

## Regression

Regression follows the same logic as advancement: automatic, data-driven, not personal.

Members who drop below threshold in a single cycle receive a warning. Members who remain below threshold in the following cycle are regressed. Members who continue below threshold after regression are reviewed for removal.

This is not punitive. It is the system maintaining the integrity of the tiers. A tier that does not reflect current contribution is a lie the club is telling itself.

## Appealing a Decision

Advancement and regression decisions can be appealed to Super Peers within one week of the cycle closing. Appeals must be based on data errors, not on disagreement with the thresholds themselves.
