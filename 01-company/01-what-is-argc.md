# What is ARGC

**ARGC — Alliance of Resilient Giveback Coders** — is an engineering alliance at 42 Amman. Its purpose is to develop exceptional engineers: people with deep technical understanding, disciplined practice, and a habit of giving back what they learn.


## The Name

- **Alliance** — a set of engineers who hold each other to a standard, not a hierarchy of titles.
- **Resilient** — the ability to stay with a difficult problem, learn from failure, and keep improving.
- **Give back** — knowledge that stops at one person has not been used.
- **Coders** — the work is real. Code, tests, systems, and infrastructure that other people depend on.

## Where It Came From

ARGC originated as the Give Back Society Club inside 42 Amman. Its founding premise was narrow and correct: students who learn something worth sharing should share it systematically rather than by accident.

The Give Back Society Club has since been structurally reshaped. The intent has not changed — the ambition around it has. Giving back inside a curriculum is a habit. Giving back inside a demanding engineering culture is a system, and a system can be designed, measured, and improved.

## Why It Exists as a Separate Initiative

ARGC is not a course, a certificate, or a department. It is an additional layer of standards, peer accountability, and engineering practice that sits alongside the 42 Amman curriculum rather than replacing it.

Members remain 42 students. They complete the same projects, the same evaluations, and the same core work. What ARGC adds is a peer group that holds a higher bar, an engineering culture built around understanding rather than completion, and a structure that turns individual growth into collective capability.

## Where It Stands

ARGC is currently in its first phase: seven Nodes of five members each, working toward the 42 project `ft_transcendence`. See [Phase I](./09-phase-i.md) for what that phase involves, and [The ARGC Journey](./08-the-argc-journey.md) for how it leads to the stages after it.

ARGC is not yet a research center, a company, a startup ecosystem, or an open-source organization. Those belong to [The Future of ARGC](./12-the-future-of-argc.md), which describes a direction and not a current state.
