# Vision

**Building an engineering culture capable of creating what does not yet exist.**

ARGC's long-term vision is to become a recognizable engineering and technological force originating from Jordan — one capable of producing work with regional relevance and, where the work warrants it, global relevance.

## The Destination

A resilient community of engineers capable of moving from learning existing technologies to understanding, designing, researching, and building new ones.

An independent engineering ecosystem where talented people can collaborate on meaningful technical problems, conduct research, develop deep-tech solutions, create open-source technologies, and contribute knowledge and engineering capacity to the wider region.

## What That Ecosystem May Include

- engineering teams working on sustained technical problems;
- open-source projects maintained in public;
- research and development work;
- deep-tech development;
- technological infrastructure built and operated by ARGC;
- partnerships with other technical organizations; and
- startups that emerge from genuine engineering and research work.

None of these exist yet. They describe the shape of the destination, not the state of the present.

## The Transition

ARGC grows from a community that **develops engineers** into an ecosystem where those engineers **develop technology**.

The second half cannot be rushed. It is earned by what the first half actually produces — which is why [Phase I](./09-phase-i.md) is about standards, evaluation, and applied work rather than organizational expansion.
