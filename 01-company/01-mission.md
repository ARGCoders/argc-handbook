# Mission

ARGC exists for one reason: to make contribution the norm at 42 Amman.

## What We Are Here to Do

The strongest engineers at 42 Amman should not hoard their advantage. They should multiply it. ARGC is the structure that makes giving back systematic — not optional, not occasional, not performed for optics, but built into how you advance.

We exist to prove that a collective of engineers committed to lifting those around them will outperform any group of individuals optimizing only for themselves.

## What Success Looks Like

- Contribution is the default behavior of the strongest students at 42 Amman, not an exception.
- The club outlives its founders. The system runs without the people who built it.
- Every node produces engineers who are stronger than when they arrived — technically and in their capacity to help others.
- ARGC is recognized by 42 Amman administration and the broader student body as a legitimate, high-standard engineering collective.

## What We Are Not

We are not a social club. We are not a networking group. We are not a résumé line.

ARGC is a production-oriented collective. If you are here, you are expected to produce.
