# Phase I — Transcendence

Phase I is the current phase. It is where ARGC exists now: 35 engineers, organized into seven Nodes, working inside 42 Amman toward a single shared engineering target.

## Current Facts

- There are currently **7 Nodes**.
- Each Node contains **5 members**.
- Total current Node membership is therefore **35 people**.
- The Nodes were intentionally formed around the new **42 Amman Core**.
- The Nodes are working toward the 42 project **`ft_transcendence`**.
- The current Node structure is **temporary**.

Phase I ends with the completion of `ft_transcendence`. It is not defined as a fixed four-month period — the project marks the end of the phase, not a calendar date.

## What Phase I Is

Phase I is not an attempt to operate a research and development center. It is not a separate program, and it does not replace the 42 curriculum.

Members remain 42 students working through the 42 curriculum. What changes is the environment around them. ARGC raises the standard of that environment — the quality of evaluation, the demand placed on understanding, and the expectation that a member gives more than the minimum.

The initial objective is therefore **internal development and cultural impact**, while laying the foundation for the stages that follow.

## What Phase I Focuses On

- **Stronger evaluation standards** — honest, specific, and held to a higher bar than the curriculum requires.
- **A more demanding engineering culture** — where the minimum result is a floor rather than a target.
- **Competitions** — a way to apply curriculum concepts under pressure, and to raise the level of the people taking part.
- **Practical application of concepts** — using what is learned at 42 on problems where the correct answer is not already given.
- **Collaboration** — working collectively toward a shared target rather than individually toward a completion criterion.
- **Leadership through Node leaders** — see [The Node Model](./10-node-model.md).
- **Raising the level of members through each iteration** — every cycle should leave the people in it stronger than the cycle before.

## Competitions and the Wider 42 Community

ARGC competitions may be open to non-ARGC students when that is appropriate. Including students outside ARGC is useful: it lets ARGC raise the standard of the wider 42 Amman environment rather than only its own, and it gives members the experience of competing against people whose work they have not reviewed.

## What Phase I Is Not Responsible For

Phase I carries no separate project portfolio, no separate curriculum, and no separate research agenda. Its work is the 42 curriculum, done to a higher standard, together.
