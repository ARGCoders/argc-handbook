# Values

**Build. Elevate. Leave a Legacy.**

These are not aspirations. They are the operating principles behind every decision ARGC makes — who gets in, how members advance, what gets built, and what gets cut.

---

## 1. Output Over Decoration

Every decision earns its place by serving the work, the data, or the mission — not by making things look impressive. We measure by what ships, not by how much effort was performed.

This applies to the platform, to peer evaluations, to events, and to how we communicate.

## 2. The Standard Is the Design

ARGC holds its members to a higher bar than the minimum. Half-done is not done. Approximate is not precise. We do not ship incomplete things and call them finished.

If the standard feels uncomfortable, that is the point.

## 3. Collective, Not Individual

The unit of ARGC is the node, not the person. Individual metrics exist to serve the collective — they are not a leaderboard for personal branding. Cross-node collaboration is expected, not optional.

Your XP reflects what you gave, not what you took.

## 4. Weight Carries Meaning

The crimson and navy. The name. The covenant. None of it is decoration.

When ARGC takes a position, produces output, or endorses something, it carries weight because we have been disciplined about what we attach that weight to. We do not dilute it by being everywhere or saying everything.

## 5. Outlive the Moment

We build for what comes after us. Every system, process, and standard is designed to run without its creator. Founding members who build things that collapse when they leave have failed at the most important part of the job.

Legacy is the final output.
