# The ARGC Journey

ARGC is being built progressively. The structure used during one phase is not the structure that will exist after it, and the organization that exists today is not the organization being aimed at.

The journey has three phases. The exact structure of the later ones will be determined by what the earlier ones actually produce.

## Phase I — Transcendence

The initial phase takes place entirely inside the 42 Amman environment. The community is organized into seven Nodes, and those Nodes work toward the 42 project `ft_transcendence`.

The objective is not to replace the curriculum with ARGC projects. It is to transform how members approach the curriculum — to change the standard, not the syllabus.

The central objective of this phase is **Transcendence**. See [Phase I](./09-phase-i.md).

## Phase II — Dissolution & Reformation

The initial Node structure is temporary by design. Its purpose is to create a strong foundation of individuals, relationships, technical ability, and shared culture.

Once `ft_transcendence` is completed, the current Nodes are dissolved. This is intentional. The structure is not failing when it ends — it is finishing what it was built to do.

After the dissolution, ARGC reorganizes around a model suited to its next stage: collaborative engineering, research, and development. What that model looks like is not decided now. It will be determined by what Phase I actually produced.

## Phase III — Engineering, Research & Development

The long-term direction is an ecosystem capable of moving beyond individual learning. This may include:

- research groups;
- engineering squads;
- open-source projects;
- deep-tech development;
- technical infrastructure;
- partnerships;
- regional initiatives;
- and other forms of engineering and scientific contribution.

Phase III is not a predefined organizational structure. It is a destination that must be **earned through the capabilities, experience, and lessons developed during the earlier phases**.
