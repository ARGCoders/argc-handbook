# Transcendence

`ft_transcendence` is the major shared objective around which the current Nodes operate. It is the engineering target of Phase I.

## What It Gives the Structure

A temporary organizational structure needs a reason to exist. `ft_transcendence` is that reason: one concrete, demanding, shared project that every Node is accountable for, and that gives the standard something to point at.

Without a shared target, seven groups of five would be thirty-five people doing separate work in the same building. With it, they are one organization pursuing one objective.

## What It Asks of Members

It asks members to apply the concepts they are learning at 42 to a project that is larger, more demanding, and more ambiguous than a curriculum exercise — and to do so together, under evaluation, to a standard higher than the assignment requires.

The project is the vehicle. The capability being built through it is the product.

## What It Is Not

`ft_transcendence` is a 42 project. Its specification belongs to 42, not to ARGC, and ARGC does not control or redefine it.

What ARGC controls is the environment around the work: the standard members are held to, the quality of evaluation, the demands added on top, and the way members work together while producing it.

## Why Completing It Matters Structurally

Completion of `ft_transcendence` marks the transition point. The current Nodes are temporary structures that exist to build a foundation of people, relationships, technical ability, and shared culture. When the project is complete, that foundation is either there or it is not.

After completion, the Nodes are dissolved and ARGC moves toward its next organizational model. See [The ARGC Journey](./08-the-argc-journey.md).
