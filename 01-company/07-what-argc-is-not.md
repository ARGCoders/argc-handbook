# What ARGC Is Not

ARGC is not:

- A social club built around events and attendance.
- A certificate-collecting organization.
- A conventional university club.
- A place where participation alone means achievement.
- A marketing organization.
- An organization that exists only to organize events.
- A hierarchy designed around titles.
- A community where membership alone represents achievement.
- A place where activity is valued more than contribution.
- An organization that expects members to already be experts.

## On Events Specifically

Events are not forbidden, and they are not unimportant. A knowledge session, a competition, or a hackathon that raises the technical level of the people who attend is a legitimate ARGC activity.

What ARGC is not is an event organization. An event that exists in order to have an event is not worth running, regardless of attendance. The test is whether it contributed to someone's engineering capability or to the organization's output.

## On Participation

Being present is not the same as contributing. Attendance is not achievement. A member who attends everything and contributes nothing has done less than a member who misses some sessions and materially raises the standard of the work around them.

## On Certification

ARGC issues no certificates and confers no titles. There is nothing to collect, and nothing that can be inherited, bought, or assumed. The only thing that can be claimed is demonstrated capability — and that is demonstrated by the work, not by membership.

ARGC is built for people who are willing to **become** better engineers, not people who want to be recognized as them.
