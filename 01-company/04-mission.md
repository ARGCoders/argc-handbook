# Mission

ARGC's mission is to develop exceptional engineers, and the engineering culture that sustains them.

## What the Mission Requires

- **Develop deep technical ability.** Depth over familiarity — the ability to reason about a system, not just operate a tool.
- **Build a demanding engineering culture.** Standards real enough that meeting them means something.
- **Encourage research and experimentation.** Applying what is learned to problems that have not been solved yet, and being willing to fail at them.
- **Create useful technological work.** Work that other people can use, run, and depend on.
- **Share knowledge.** Documenting, teaching, and reviewing treated as core engineering duties rather than as extras.
- **Give back.** Every member is both a learner and a contributor; the growth of one member should strengthen the people around them.
- **Build systems that let capable people work together.** Structure, standards, and tools that make collaboration productive rather than merely possible.

## How the Mission Is Pursued

During Phase I, the mission is pursued primarily through the 42 Amman curriculum. Members continue their normal 42 journey while ARGC provides an additional layer of standards, challenges, peer development, and accountability.

The mission is a direction, not a task list. Evaluations, competitions, events, and contributions are instruments. What matters is the capability they produce in people, and the work that capability eventually builds.
