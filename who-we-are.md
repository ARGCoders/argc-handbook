# Who We Are

ARGC (Alliance of Resilient Giveback Coders) is a production-oriented engineering collective operating inside 42 Amman.

## Membership

ARGC is not open enrollment. Membership is earned.

Members are selected from the 42 Amman student body based on two criteria:
1. **Technical strength** — demonstrated through 42 project history and peer evaluation scores.
2. **Disposition to help others** — demonstrated through visible contribution to the student community before being considered.

The club is intentionally small. Quality of contribution matters more than size of membership.

## Structure

ARGC is organized into **nodes** — small, focused groups of engineers with a defined domain of responsibility. Nodes are the primary unit of work and accountability. You belong to a node before you belong to ARGC.

### Roles

| Role | Responsibility |
|------|---------------|
| **Member** | Active contributor within a node. Completes evaluations, attends events, accumulates XP. |
| **Node Leader** | Manages evaluations and internal operations within their node. Accountable for node output. |
| **Super Peer** | Oversees club-wide metrics, voting data, and cross-node operations. Highest tier of membership. |

## Advancement

Members advance through a tiered system based on XP and contribution activity — not seniority, not popularity, not time served.

XP is earned through:
- Peer evaluations completed
- Events organized or led
- Cross-node contributions
- External endorsements and community output

Advancement is reviewed periodically. Regression is possible. The tier is a reflection of current contribution, not past achievement.

## Who Can See This

The platform has two surfaces:

- **Member surface** — restricted to active ARGC members. Tracks XP, node progress, evaluations, and voting.
- **Public surface** — visible to the full 42 student body and administration. Communicates what ARGC is, why it matters, and how to get involved.

This handbook is part of the public surface.
