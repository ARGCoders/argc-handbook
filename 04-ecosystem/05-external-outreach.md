# External Outreach

External outreach is how ARGC connects its work to audiences outside 42 Amman. This includes collaborations with other schools, industry contacts, and public output that represents the club. Everything that goes out under the ARGC name is held to the same standard as internal work — we do not lower the bar because the audience is external.

## Why It Exists

ARGC's mission is not contained within 42 Amman. The culture of contribution we are building here should be visible, replicable, and recognized beyond our campus.

External outreach serves two purposes:
1. **Legitimacy** — the more the outside world sees ARGC's output, the more weight the name carries for the members who built it.
2. **Accountability** — committing to external audiences raises the standard of internal work. We do not want to show something publicly we would be embarrassed to explain.

## What Counts as External Outreach

- Presenting ARGC's work to another school, company, or organization
- Publishing technical content (articles, repos, documentation) that references ARGC
- Partnering with external entities on a project or event
- Speaking at or hosting events that include non-42 Amman participants
- Any public communication on behalf of the club (social media, newsletters, announcements)

## The Standard

Everything produced under the ARGC name must meet the same bar as internal work:
- Accurate and honest
- Complete — not a half-finished artifact presented as finished
- Reviewed by at least one Super Peer before it is released

ARGC does not overpromise. If a partnership would require us to commit to something we cannot currently deliver, we decline or defer.

## How to Initiate Outreach

1. **Write a proposal** — what you want to do, who the external party is, what ARGC's deliverable would be, and what you expect in return.
2. **Submit to Super Peers** — all external outreach requires Super Peer sign-off before any commitment is made.
3. **Execute with a record** — every external engagement produces a written record: what happened, who was involved, and what was produced or agreed.

Members who initiate and complete successful external outreach with documented outcomes earn high XP. The emphasis is on "documented outcomes" — outreach that does not produce a record did not happen.
