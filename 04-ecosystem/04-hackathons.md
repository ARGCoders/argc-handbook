# Hackathons

ARGC hackathons are time-boxed, output-focused events where nodes build something real under constraint. They are not team-building exercises. The deliverable is a working artifact — code, documentation, a system — that either ships or becomes the foundation for something that does.

## What They Are

A hackathon in ARGC is a constrained build event. The constraint is time. The output is a real artifact.

"Real" means: code that runs, documentation that covers something the club needs, a system design that will be used. It does not mean a prototype that exists only to be demonstrated at the event. If you are not willing to continue the work after the event, the work does not belong in a hackathon.

## Format

| Element | Detail |
|---------|--------|
| **Duration** | 4–12 hours (set by organizer at proposal) |
| **Team size** | 2–4 members |
| **Deliverable** | Defined at the start; submitted at the end |
| **Judging** | Node Leaders review deliverables within 48 hours |
| **Record** | Pushed to platform within 24 hours of close |

Cross-node teams are encouraged. Teams that mix domains tend to produce more durable work.

## The Rules

**Define the deliverable before you start.** A hackathon without a defined deliverable is a work session with a deadline — useful, but not a hackathon.

**Submit what you have.** Incomplete work is submitted as incomplete, with a documented gap analysis. Do not delete your work because it did not reach the original scope. The artifact and the gap analysis together are more valuable than nothing.

**No solo hackathons.** The point is cross-pollination. Solo build sessions are valid contribution activity but are logged as individual contribution, not as a hackathon.

## After the Event

- Deliverables are reviewed by Node Leaders and Super Peers
- Work that is production-quality is merged or deployed
- Work that has potential is assigned to a node for continuation
- Work that does not meet the bar is archived with a written assessment — not discarded silently

## XP

Hackathon participation carries high XP weight. Organizers earn more than participants. Participants who submit a deliverable earn more than participants who do not. Attending without submitting earns attendance credit only.
