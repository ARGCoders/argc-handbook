# Vision

## The Future We Are Building Toward

42 Amman produces engineers who do not just pass — they elevate everyone around them.

ARGC's vision is a 42 Amman where the culture of contribution is self-sustaining. Where the strongest students do not graduate and disappear — they leave behind a system, a standard, and a generation of engineers who carry that standard forward.

## What That Looks Like in Practice

- A tiered advancement structure that outlives every founding member.
- A contribution record that is visible, measurable, and tied to real outcomes — not just activity.
- Cross-node collaboration as the default, not the exception.
- ARGC alumni recognizable at the industry level not because of where they studied, but because of how they work.

## The Long Game

Most clubs die with their founders. ARGC is designed to outlast them.

Every system we build, every process we document, every standard we enforce is done with one question in mind: **will this still run without us?**

If the answer is no, we are not done yet.
